1. Information We Collect

The information collected depends on the features you use.

1.1 Account and Profile Information

When you register or maintain a SettleMitr account, we may collect:

  • Your name;
  • Email address;
  • Mobile number;
  • Password in securely processed or hashed form;
  • Profile picture, where provided;
  • User ID and account identifiers;
  • UPI ID, where voluntarily added;
  • Account preferences, including language, appearance and notification settings.

SettleMitr does not ask for or store your UPI PIN, banking password, debit-card PIN or one-time password.

1.2 Expense and Group Information

When you create or participate in expenses, groups or personal records, we may process:

  • Group names and descriptions;
  • Group members and administrators;
  • Expense titles, categories, amounts and currencies;
  • Items, quantities, discounts, taxes and adjustments;
  • Split amounts, shares and allocation rules;
  • Payer and receiver details;
  • Advances, balances, settlements and payment status;
  • Expense dates, notes and comments;
  • Tags, reminders and activity history;
  • Group messages or chat content;
  • Uploaded receipts, bills and supporting files;
  • Exported or shared expense statements.

This information is necessary to calculate balances and provide SettleMitr’s expense-management features.

1.3 Contacts Information

With your permission, SettleMitr may access contacts stored on your device so that you can find or add people to groups and expenses. Depending on the feature used, we may process selected contact names, mobile numbers and email addresses.

SettleMitr will not intentionally upload your entire address book unless necessary for a feature clearly disclosed to you. You may deny or withdraw contact permission through your device settings.

Before adding another person’s information, you should ensure that you have permission to use it for expense coordination.

1.4 Bills, Receipts, Camera and Files

When you photograph or upload a bill, SettleMitr may process:

  • The photograph or uploaded document;
  • Merchant or vendor name;
  • Item descriptions;
  • Quantities and prices;
  • Taxes, discounts, totals and dates;
  • Other text visible in the document.

SettleMitr may use optical character recognition or similar technology to extract information from a bill. Automatically extracted information may be incomplete or inaccurate. You should review it before saving an expense.

Camera, photo-library and file permissions are used only when you choose the related feature.

1.5 UPI and Settlement Information

SettleMitr may help generate a UPI payment request or redirect you to a compatible UPI application.

SettleMitr may process:

  • The recipient’s UPI ID;
  • Payment amount and description;
  • Transaction reference provided by the payment application;
  • Payment success, failure, pending or cancellation status, where returned;
  • Manually recorded settlement information.

The payment itself is processed by your bank, UPI application, payment service provider or another third party. SettleMitr does not independently access or control your bank account.

1.6 Communications and Notifications

We may collect information relating to:

  • Expense reminders;
  • Group invitations;
  • Settlement requests;
  • Comments and mentions;
  • In-app notifications;
  • Push-notification tokens;
  • Support requests and feedback.

When you share information through WhatsApp, email, SMS or another external application, that service will process the information according to its own privacy policy.

1.7 Device and Technical Information

Depending on the version of the Service and the services enabled, we may automatically collect:

  • Device type and operating-system version;
  • App version;
  • Language and time-zone settings;
  • Device or installation identifiers;
  • IP address;
  • Login timestamps;
  • App interactions;
  • Error logs, crash reports and performance information;
  • Security and fraud-prevention signals.

SettleMitr does not require precise location information for its core expense-management functions.
